Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

About this application

BTC Fortress rclone is a personal utility used by the owner to synchronize files between Google Drive and private computers/VPS environments.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive data only as necessary to synchronize, upload, download, and back up files requested by the owner.

Use of data

Google user data is used only for personal file synchronization and backup operations.

Storage of data

Files may be stored on the owner's private computers, VPS environments, and Google Drive as part of the synchronization process.

Sharing of data

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Access and security

Access to Google Drive is performed through OAuth authentication. OAuth credentials and tokens are used only to operate the owner's personal synchronization environment.

Data deletion

The owner may delete synchronized files at any time from Google Drive or from the private systems where copies are stored.

Contact

This application is for personal use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.
